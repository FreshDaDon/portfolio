Research interests.

My research interests sit at the intersection of human behaviour, artificial intelligence, learning technologies and computational methods. I am particularly interested in using behavioural and educational data to understand how people interact with intelligent and digital systems.

01

Psychoinformatics

The computational study of behavioural and psychological phenomena using digital interaction data, statistical modelling and machine learning.
